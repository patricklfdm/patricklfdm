Wayvia is an AI-powered travel planning platform that generates personalized itineraries through multi-stage reasoning, semantic preference matching, and asynchronous workflow orchestration.

## Engineering areas

- **Planning:** LLM-based reasoning, vector search, semantic preference matching, and multi-stage planning pipelines.
- **Workflow orchestration:** queue-based asynchronous processing and WebSocket progress updates.
- **Application and state:** Next.js/React and NestJS, with PostgreSQL and Redis state management and interactive itinerary visualization.
- **Operations:** AWS deployment, Docker, CI/CD, reliability, and observability.
